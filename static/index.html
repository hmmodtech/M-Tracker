About the Platform
M-TRACKER (Media Tracker) is an interactive, intelligent dashboard designed for real-time monitoring of the latest security trends, humanitarian developments, and field intelligence across the Gaza Strip and the broader Palestine/Levant conflict zone.
The platform was purpose-built for Action Against Hunger (ACF) — Occupied Palestinian Territory (oPt), Gaza Base to empower field coordinators, security assistants, and information management officers with faster, smarter situational awareness — replacing manual monitoring of dozens of live sources with a single unified, automated intelligence feed.
M-TRACKER aggregates content from 70+ Telegram channels and open-source news websites, applies Arabic and English keyword intelligence, automatically geolocates events to a 2,337-record Gaza Strip GIS database (UNOCHA/OCHA/OSM), and delivers real-time critical alerts — making it an indispensable tool for humanitarian security monitoring in one of the world's most complex operational environments.
